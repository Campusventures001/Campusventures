Our model, not a marketplace

We Are Not a Middleman

CampusVentures buys schools and colleges directly. We own the institution throughout the entire cycle — from acquisition to improvement to exit. We don't just introduce two parties and collect a fee.

Step 1

We own the school

We acquire the institution outright using our own capital. Full ownership, land and operating rights transfer to us.

Step 2

We fix & improve

Over 12–24 months we turn around enrollment, branding, curriculum, faculty and facilities — with our own team and investment.

Step 3

We resell or franchise

Once the institution is performing, we exit by selling outright, franchising the brand, or keeping it as a cash-flow asset.

Questions

Before you list or inquire

How does CampusVentures buy schools?+
We acquire institutions directly using our own capital — full ownership, land and operating rights transfer to us. There is no marketplace or broker involved. We identify undervalued schools, negotiate directly with the owner, and close the acquisition ourselves.
What happens after you buy a school?+
Over 12–24 months we fix enrollment, branding, curriculum, faculty and facilities. Once the institution is performing, we either sell it outright, franchise the brand, or hold it as a cash-flow asset. We own it through the entire cycle.
Can a school owner still get a good price?+
Yes. We pay fair market value based on land, enrollment, board affiliation and financials. The owner gets a clean exit with a single counterparty — no listing fees, no waiting for buyers, no deal fall-throughs.
Do you lease or manage schools too?+
Primarily we buy. In select cases we also lease campuses or take over management, but our core model is direct ownership. We take the risk, make the investment, and capture the upside ourselves.
Who are your investors?+
We are privately backed by a group of education and real estate investors. The same approach used by Apollo, Blackstone and GEMS at scale — applied to the Indian school market at a more manageable size, starting with one school at a time.
